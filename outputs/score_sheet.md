Maximum score: 35.

On 50,738 held-out survey respondents, flagging at 18 or more flagged 34.0% of people, caught 81.2% of those reporting heart disease, and 22.5% of flagged people reported heart disease.

The outcome is self-reported history of coronary heart disease or heart attack in a US telephone survey. The sheet has not been validated in a clinic or against clinical diagnosis.
